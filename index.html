Good questions

Before we get started.

A few practical answers to help you decide whether to reach out.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

We’re building our focus around startups and growing businesses that need a thoughtful brand, a stronger website, or a useful digital product. We’ll discuss fit and scope during the first conversation.

Can you build both web and mobile applications?

Yes. We offer web application and mobile application development, alongside branding, UI/UX, and website development. The right approach depends on the users, requirements, budget, and long-term plans.

How much does a project cost?

Costs depend on scope, complexity, integrations, and timeline. After understanding your goals, we can discuss a suitable scope and provide a clear proposal before work begins.

Do you work with clients internationally?

Gridline Digital is positioning itself to work with clients in markets including the US, UK, Canada, and Australia. Project fit, communication, and delivery expectations will be agreed upfront.

What if I only have an idea?

You don’t need every detail figured out. Bring the idea and what you hope it will achieve; the discovery conversation can help clarify the next step.
